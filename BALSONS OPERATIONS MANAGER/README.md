# BALSONS Operations Manager
## Heavy Erection Planning, Crane Mobilization & Shift Execution Portal

**Anchor Project:** Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory (JTRWF), Khordha, Odisha  
**Architecture Standard:** `BALSONS-SAM-2026` / `BEDS V1.0`  
**Governance Policy:** `Global Super Administrator Policy (Version 1.0)`  

### Module Scope & Capabilities:
- Heavy lift planning and tandem crane rigging calculations
- Hydraulic puller and multi-axle trailer mobilization
- Schuler wheel forging press line erection tracking
- Shift handover logbook and site coordination
- Inter-synchronized with Gate Entry, Store, HR, and Safety portals
