🛠️ Mechanical Scope of Work (SOW) Boundaries (§05 Specification)

Enforced Contractual Scope Baseline

Machine Shop BALSONS mechanical scope is strictly bounded to agreed erection activities. Hard exclusions require formal client commercial variation before execution:

✔ BALSONS Mechanical Scope (Included Baseline)

  • 1. Unloading & controlled receipt: Lifting from transport trailers onto laydown area.
  • 2. Unpacking & component identification: De-crating, tag cross-verification with packing lists.
  • 3. Cleaning & preparation: Anti-rust coating degreasing and surface wiping.
  • 4. Internal shifting: Rigging and moving machine assemblies near/on foundation pockets.
  • 5. Mechanical installation: Placement on base plates and mounting bolts.
  • 6. Rough levelling: Initial spirit/laser levelling to within ±1.0 mm tolerance.
  • 7. X-Y axis positioning: Centerline alignment with bay coordinates and benchmarks.

⛔ Strict Exclusions (Client / OEM Scope — No Proxy Work)

  • 1. Electrical installation: Cabling, cable tray laying, panel wiring, terminations.
  • 2. PLC / CNC integration: Automation bus wiring, fieldbus setup, CNC program loading.
  • 3. Civil / foundation work: Foundation casting, core cutting, pocket chipping, grouting.
  • 4. Final precision levelling: Sub-micron geometric certification and laser interferometer checks.
  • 5. Calibration & OEM commissioning: Spindle balancing, sensor tuning, OEM cold run.
  • 6. Production trials: Live machining, chip-making, CPK/PPK statistical acceptance.
  • 7. Unapproved scope: Any work not explicitly recorded in written SOW variation.

5. ALLOCATED: Assigned to named work package, shift, date range.
